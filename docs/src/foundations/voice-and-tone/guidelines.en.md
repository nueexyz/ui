# Voice and Tone

Write short, specific sentences without blaming the user. Keep these principles even when a product has its own voice.

## Match the situation

| Situation           | Approach                                 |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Routine guidance    | Lead with the information needed         |
| Success             | Briefly confirm what finished            |
| Error               | Explain the problem and next action      |
| Irreversible action | Clearly state the target and consequence |

Avoid exaggerated celebration and long apologies that delay the solution. Translate internal technical language into words that help the user.

## Examples

- Prefer: “Changes saved.”
- Prefer: “Could not save. Check your connection and try again.”
- Avoid: “Unknown error code!”

Keep formality and sentence style consistent on one screen. For legal consent or financial consequences, prioritize precision and have the product owner verify the wording.
