## Usage guidelines

Group related sections under descriptive headings. Keep more than one open when people need to compare answers.

> Keep required instructions visible instead of hiding them in a closed section.

### Composition and styling

Use Accordion, AccordionItem, AccordionTrigger, and AccordionContent. The trigger
includes its heading, and content includes its animated panel. Content `xstyle`
applies to the inner element directly wrapping children. `keepMounted`,
`hiddenUntilFound`, `ref`, and other DOM props apply to the outer panel.
